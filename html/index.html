About us

The Grosse Ile Parent And Teacher (PAT) Booster Club is a membership organization of Elementary, Middle School and High School Parents, Teachers, and Administrators. Our common goal is to raise funds to finance supplemental enrichment programming and other items in all three of our schools which are deemed important by the membership with the advisement of our Principals.


We provide supplementary resources for a wide variety of programs, including:



Classroom technologyLiteracy Night

Holiday Gift Shop for the elementary schoolsSummer Literacy

Support for specific grade eventsClassroom supplies

Spirit WearGuest Author Visits

Enrichment ActivitiesFamily enrichment nights

Teacher/Principal requestsTeacher Appreciation

Walk and Bike to School prizesSpecial events funding

Lending Libraries and Book Vending Machine books

and more!

Our Mission
  • To develop between educators of our township and the general united efforts to secure for every child the highest advantages in physical, mental, and social education.
  • The PAT Booster Club is proud to support our kids, our teachers, and our schools in practical and enriching ways. We provide enrichment programs to supplement your child’s educational experience as well as wish list items which may not be provided for in the district’s annual budget.
  • PAT accomplishes this through the tireless work of many volunteers and committees which make up the Grosse Ile PAT.
